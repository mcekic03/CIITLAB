CIITLab (Computational Intelligence and Information Technologies Lab) is a part of the Department of Computer Science at the Faculty of Electronic Engineering, University of Niš.

About CIITLab

The CIITLab Laboratory conducts research across several key domains within information technology, including Natural Language Processing, Spectral Techniques, Web Development, and Computer Chess.

The Laboratory has actively participated in the implementation of numerous research projects financed by the Ministry of Science and Technological Development, as well as in internationally funded programs such as DAAD and TEMPUS.

CIITLab has developed strong international collaborations, which are reflected in the organization of multiple summer schools in the field of Computational Intelligence, along with numerous seminars and workshops featuring a significant number of distinguished international professors.

Of particular note is the international meeting "Dyadic Walsh and Analysis", held at the Faculty of Electronic Engineering in Niš in 2007, which led to the publication of a monograph of the same title.

CIITLab also initiated electronic publishing at the Faculty of Electronic Engineering, within which several electronic publications registered with the National Library of Serbia were published.
